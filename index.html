    Break slots

    Paste your slot list, one slot per line, in the order you sold them. Emojis, prices and numbering are ignored. Ahri + Fizz (or / & , and) is a combo slot. Words like Epic, Holo Gear, Alt Art Runes work too. A slot that names a card (a champion, "Seals") beats a broad one ("Gear", "Epic"); otherwise the higher line wins. Check the counts below the box.

    Exact rules (advanced)

    Use name = terms for exact rules. Terms are separated by commas (any of them); & means "all of" here, and ! means "not". Filters: tag: name: domain: type: rarity: set: number: holo.
    Zaun crew = ekko, viktor, tag:zaun
    Jinx (no legend) = jinx & !type:legend
    Gear = type:gear & holo: the camera can't see holo, so the scanner asks "Holo?" when it matters.
